# A Machine's Loaded Recipe is the recipe DYE2 has selected, recorded in the Workflow, and followed both ways

A Recipe is the day's dial for one roast, which baristas keep current through the day. When a barista on one Machine moves the Brazil's grind from 2.4 to 2.5, every other Machine with the Brazil loaded has to take that at once: the grinder is physically at 2.5, and their next Shots would otherwise record 2.4. The owner also counts which Recipe a Machine has loaded as part of its state, for analytics.

## DYE2's selection

From v0.1.20, DYE2 keeps one selected recipe or favourite on each tablet (`dye2:dye2-plugin/src/utils/workflow-record.ts`). DYE2's dashboard and Streamline's DYE strip (from v0.2.14) select what they apply: they write the Workflow, then post the item to `POST /api/v1/plugins/dye2.reaplugin/selection`, which DYE2 answers from memory at `GET` on the same path, with its kind, id and the fingerprint of the profile it was selected on. Decaid answers 404 there while DYE2 is not loaded, as while it reloads or updates.

- **When DYE2 clears it.** On a change of the Workflow's profile, by its own fingerprint (title, target weight and volume, tank temperature and steps, but each step's temperature), so a yield Streamline writes into the profile's target weight clears it too; on a recent favourite applied; and on `POST /selection {}`. Not when the selected recipe is removed from its list.
- **What DYE2 keeps.** The selection is kept in DYE2's plugin storage, and restored as DYE2 loads. A cleared one is stored as `null`, which Decaid's store doesn't keep, so the selection cleared comes back.
- **When DYE2 pushes.** `{"what":"workflow"}` on its `changed` socket when it selects an item, after it records, and after it refreshes its recent favourites, as it loads and after each Shot. Never when the selection clears.
- **What DYE2 records.** While a recipe is selected, on every change of the Workflow, whatever made it, DYE2 records into its record in `recipes` the Workflow's value of each field the record holds that differs: the dose, yield and grind setting, the steam and hot water values of the recipe's active modes and the flush time, and the brew temperature once the first step's temperature moves off the one it had when selected. It ignores the Workflow its own selection was made on, for 3 s.
- **DYE2's dashboard copy.** DYE2's dashboard reads the Workflow once, as it opens, and sends that whole copy back with each dial change and on Done.

## The Loaded Recipe

When a tablet's DYE2 selection names a recipe in a slot that holds one of its Location's Recipes, and was made on the profile the Workflow holds now, that Recipe is the Machine's Loaded Recipe. The plugin compares the selection's fingerprint with DYE2's fingerprint of the Workflow's profile, DYE2's own test of a profile change; a selection with no fingerprint matches. A selection made on another profile, as one DYE2 restored after the profile changed, loads nothing, and DYE2 clears it at the Workflow's next change. A favourite selected, or nothing, loads none.

The plugin reads the selection from DYE2's endpoint on each push from DYE2's `changed` socket, about 2 s after each change of the Workflow (past DYE2's 1.5 s wait before it records), to catch the clears DYE2 does not push, and on its poll. A 404 tells nothing, and changes nothing. On every read and every change of the Workflow, the plugin keeps the Workflow's context naming the Loaded Recipe, or none (`decentSyncRecipeId`), so Decaid copies it onto every Shot pulled with it. The first Workflow the plugin sees as it loads clears nothing: a barista may have selected something since.

On a load, the plugin writes into the Workflow what applying a DYE2 recipe leaves out, or a stale copy got wrong: the Recipe's current dial, its brew temperature, its Bean Batch, grinder, Basket and RPM, and all its hot water settings (ADR-0014). A brew temperature is written as Streamline's Brew tile writes one (`shiftedTemperatures` in `streamline-js:src/modules/brew-temp.js`): the profile's first step goes to it, and every other step moves by the same amount. A profile with no usable first step temperature is written none. Into each recipe record, the server writes the Recipe's profile already at its brew temperature, by the same rule, in DYE2's `profileSnapshot` and the embedded Workflow, and `decentSyncRecipeId` in that Workflow's context. So DYE2's and Streamline's own applies set the brew temperature, and a recipe applied from Streamline's strip names its Recipe, at once.

A write of the plugin's that changes the Workflow's profile, as a load from a stale copy or an edit of the Recipe's Profile does, clears DYE2's selection as a profile change. So the plugin selects the Recipe's slot again right after it, as DYE2's dashboard does after an apply, and its reads between the write and the selection do not unload the Recipe.

## Following the Recipe both ways

While a Recipe is loaded, the Machine follows it both ways.

- **From the Machine.** DYE2 records a change of the dose, yield, grind setting, brew temperature, or the hot water value of the recipe's active mode into the recipe's record, and the plugin takes it in as the tablet's edit of the Recipe, as it takes in any change of a recipe record (ADR-0005, ADR-0020). For these, DYE2 is the only recorder. As DYE2 records only a field the record holds, the server makes every Recipe's record hold a brew temperature, taken from its profile's first step where the Recipe has none. The plugin reads the Workflow itself only for what DYE2 does not record: the RPM, the Basket, every hot water setting but the active mode's (all four where the record has no hot water mode), and another batch of the Recipe's Bean, as DYE2's recipes name a Bean but no batch (decentespresso/dye2#27). DYE2 records the steam and flush values too, which stay the Location's settings, taken from the Workflow as ADR-0014 says.
- **Stale values.** A part set back, whoever records it, is stale, and the Recipe's value is written back, where it is the value the tablet's record held just before the server's latest write of it (a stale save, ADR-0005), or the value the Workflow held just before the plugin's latest write of it. The second covers DYE2's dashboard sending back on Done the Workflow it read as it opened (decentespresso/dye2#28), which would undo a load, and a Workflow left behind its record, as when Decaid refused the plugin's hot water write while no machine was connected. For a field DYE2 records, the plugin compares the value DYE2 recorded with what the Workflow held before its write.
- **To other Machines.** An edit of the Recipe from anywhere is written into its record on each tablet at its Location, then into the Workflow of every Machine that has it loaded, the brew temperature as on a load. DYE2 there finds its record holding the values, and records nothing.
- **Kept in the Workflow.** Streamline clears the batch, coffee, grinder, Basket and RPM after each Shot, and as it starts (`clearDyeWorkflowContext` in `streamline-js:src/modules/dyeStrip.js`), so they do not label the next Shot. That is neither an edit nor an unload. While the Recipe is loaded, each Workflow change that clears one of them has the server write the Recipe's value back, once for that clear, so the next Shot records them: a Recipe made for a particular Basket is pulled with it on every Machine. A Shot started before the write lands records none.
- **Unloading.** When DYE2's selection clears or names something else, the Recipe is unloaded, and the Workflow names none. The plugin clears DYE2's selection (`POST /selection {}`, the call Streamline makes when its DYE strip is turned off) where it still names the slot of a Recipe that has left it: taken out of its slot, Archived, or of a Location the Machine left. A failed clear is tried again, unless DYE2 has selected something since.
- **Capture-only.** As a Machine is made capture-only, the Recipe the server last had as its Loaded Recipe is unloaded and cleared from DYE2's selection. One loaded on the tablet before the server heard of it, as while offline, or selected there afterwards, which nothing there shares, stays selected and is recorded into there, but is not its Loaded Recipe; once its sharing is turned back on, it is, where its slot holds one of its Location's Recipes. A Capture-only Machine is written nothing else, so none of the workarounds below runs on it.
- **Slots.** Moving the Recipe to another slot keeps it loaded: the plugin clears the selection before rewriting the list, and selects the new slot after, and its reads meanwhile do not unload the Recipe. So DYE2 never records into a recipe put in the old slot meanwhile.

A skin built for a Decent Sync deployment applies a recipe as Streamline does: it writes the Workflow, then selects the recipe in DYE2.

## Interim: workarounds and accepted gaps

Each workaround here does on DYE2's or Streamline's behalf what an upstream ask would have them do, and goes when they do (AGENTS.md):

- **A yield set in Streamline.** It changes the profile's target weight, which clears DYE2's selection before DYE2 records it. Where the Workflow's profile changed only in its target weight or volume, the plugin selects the Loaded Recipe's slot again, without a load's write, and takes the yield in from the Workflow as the Recipe's edit (decentespresso/dye2#18).
- **A change of grinder or Bean.** DYE2 keeps the selection through one, and would record the other grinder's or Bean's grind into the Recipe, which would reach every Machine with it loaded. So the plugin unloads the Recipe and clears DYE2's selection itself (decentespresso/dye2#19). A grinder or batch cleared is no such change.
- **The Recipe's id in the Workflow.** The plugin writes `decentSyncRecipeId` until DYE2 records its selection there (decentespresso/dye2#20). Until then, a Shot started between a recipe applied from DYE2's dashboard and the plugin's write carries the Recipe loaded before it, or none.
- **The RPM, Basket and batch** are read from the Workflow until DYE2 records them (decentespresso/dye2#21, and decentespresso/dye2#27 for the batch).
- **The brew temperature** is written on a load, and into each record's profile, until DYE2 and Streamline apply a recipe's own (decentespresso/dye2#26).
- **The batch, grinder, Basket and RPM after a Shot** are written back until Streamline leaves the selected recipe's in place (decentespresso/streamline-js#149).
- **Reading after each Workflow change** stands in for DYE2 pushing a cleared selection (decentespresso/dye2#24).
- **A cleared selection kept cleared.** When DYE2's endpoint answers no selection after naming a recipe, or the plugin clears it itself, the plugin writes `{}` to DYE2's store key `selection` (`POST /api/v1/store/dye2.reaplugin/selection`), which DYE2 restores as no selection; a slot move's own clear writes nothing there. It reads the endpoint again after, and should DYE2 have selected something meanwhile, writes that instead. Otherwise DYE2 would restore the cleared selection as it next loads, and the plugin would load the Recipe again, writing its batch and grinder back over a Bean switch (decentespresso/dye2#22).

And these gaps are accepted until fixed upstream:

- A selection cleared while the plugin was not running comes back when DYE2 reloads, and loads the Recipe again if the Workflow still holds its profile (decentespresso/dye2#22).
- Streamline's DYE strip switched off clears DYE2's selection with no Workflow change, so the plugin sees it only at its poll (decentespresso/dye2#24).
- When Streamline fails to post its selection, DYE2 records the applied recipe's dial into the one selected before, and Streamline saves later changes into the applied one itself (decentespresso/streamline-js#148).
- A Machine waking with a Streamline wake profile set is sent that profile's dose, yield, grind and brew temperature as saved on that tablet, which DYE2 then records into the Recipe, or which clears the selection where the yield differs (decentespresso/streamline-js#153). A Machine that follows Recipes is set up with no wake profile.

## Rejected

We first worked the Loaded Recipe out by matching the Workflow against the Location's Recipes, on profile, dose, yield, grinder and grind setting, and read every Workflow change while one was loaded as an edit of it. We rejected keeping that once DYE2 selected and recorded recipes itself. The two would be separate models of the same thing, which disagree: DYE2 would keep recording into a recipe the plugin had unloaded, or into another than the one it matched, and the server would send that recipe's changes to every Machine. And recording ourselves the fields DYE2 records would make two recorders of one change, which count it twice or disagree about it. We follow the apps' own model and ask upstream for what it lacks.

We rejected sharing only the stored recipes, picked up the next time a barista loads one, because Shots would record a dial the Machine no longer has. We rejected leaving the Recipe off the Workflow until DYE2 records it there, because Shots pulled while the tablet is offline would then reach the server without their Recipe.

## Consequences

- **Every tweak is the Recipe's.** A change on a Machine with a Recipe loaded to its dose, yield, grind setting, brew temperature, RPM, Basket, hot water, or batch of the Recipe's Bean changes it on every Machine at the Location. That is the intent: a dose is never changed for one customer.
- **Only an applied recipe is loaded.** Dialling in a Recipe's values by hand loads nothing. Applying a favourite while a Recipe is loaded unloads it, as DYE2 then selects the favourite.
- **DYE2 is required.** A Machine without DYE2 v0.1.20 or later has no Loaded Recipe (ADR-0017). Nothing falls back to matching. The plugin still writes the recipe lists to every sharing tablet (ADR-0005).
- **A change DYE2 skips is not the Recipe's.** DYE2 records only fields the recipe holds, so a change to one a Recipe leaves blank, but the brew temperature, stays on that Machine.
- **The plugin writes more of the tablet.** Beyond milestone 2's shared settings and the clearing on joining (ADR-0008), it writes a Loaded Recipe's values into the Workflow, DYE2's selection and its stored selection, and each recipe record's profile at the Recipe's brew temperature. A skin that remembers the hot water settings takes them as its own (ADR-0021).
- **Shots name their Recipe, and record its Basket.** Each Shot carries the Recipe its Machine had loaded, and the Recipe's batch, grinder, Basket and RPM. A Shot is linked to its Recipe by its Machine's Loaded Recipes when it was pulled, not by the id alone: a Capture-only Machine's Shots name a recipe that was never its Loaded Recipe. So milestone 5 needs no inferred recipes.
