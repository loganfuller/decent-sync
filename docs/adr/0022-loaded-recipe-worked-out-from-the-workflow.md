# A Machine's Loaded Recipe is the recipe DYE2 has selected, recorded in the Workflow, and followed both ways

A Recipe is the day's dial for one roast, which baristas keep current through the day. When a barista on one Machine moves the Brazil's grind from 2.4 to 2.5, every other Machine with the Brazil loaded has to take that at once: the grinder is physically at 2.5, and their next Shots would otherwise record 2.4. The owner also counts which Recipe a Machine has loaded as part of its state, for analytics.

## DYE2's selection

DYE2 keeps one selected recipe or favourite on each tablet (`dye2:dye2-plugin/src/utils/workflow-record.ts`, at `2cf631c`, the supported floor, ADR-0017). DYE2's dashboard and Streamline's DYE strip select what they apply: they write the Workflow, then post the item to `POST /api/v1/plugins/dye2.reaplugin/selection`, which DYE2 answers from memory at `GET` on the same path, with its kind, id, the fingerprint of the profile it was selected on, and the batch, grinder and machine settings the Workflow held then. Decaid answers 404 there while DYE2 is not loaded, as while it reloads or updates.

- **When DYE2 clears it.** On a change of the Workflow's profile, by its own fingerprint (title, tank temperature and steps, but each step's temperature, so neither a brew temperature nor a yield Streamline writes into the profile's target weight changes it, decentespresso/dye2#18); on a change of the Workflow's batch or grinder off the one it held when the item was selected, a batch or grinder cleared included (where it held none then, it takes the first one set as the one it held, decentespresso/dye2#19); on a recent favourite applied; and on `POST /selection {}`. Not when the selected recipe is removed from its list.
- **What DYE2 keeps.** The selection is kept in DYE2's plugin storage, and restored as DYE2 loads. A cleared one is stored as `{}`, which it restores as none (decentespresso/dye2#22).
- **When DYE2 pushes.** `{"what":"workflow"}` on its `changed` socket on every change of which item is selected, a clear included (decentespresso/dye2#24), after it records, and after it refreshes its recent favourites, as it loads and after each Shot.
- **What DYE2 records.** While a recipe is selected, on every change of the Workflow, whatever made it, DYE2 records into its record in `recipes` the Workflow's value of each field the record holds that differs: the dose, yield, grind setting, RPM and Basket, the steam, hot water and flush settings it holds, and the brew temperature once the first step's temperature moves off the one it had when selected. A steam, hot water or flush setting the record does not hold is recorded once it moves off the value the Workflow held when the recipe was selected. It ignores the Workflow its own selection was made on, for 3 s.
- **What DYE2 writes into the Workflow.** On each change of which item is selected, `context.extras.dye.selection`, `{kind, id}` or null, which Decaid copies onto each Shot (decentespresso/dye2#20); and with each field its dashboard sets, a flag of it in `context.extras.dye`, which Streamline's post-shot clear is scoped by.
- **DYE2's dashboard copy.** DYE2's dashboard reads the Workflow once, as it opens, and sends that whole copy back with each change made there and on Done. It reads a recipe again before applying it (decentespresso/dye2#28), but not the Workflow (decentespresso/dye2#31).

## The Loaded Recipe

When a tablet's DYE2 selection names a recipe in a slot that holds one of its Location's Recipes, and was made on the profile the Workflow holds now, that Recipe is the Machine's Loaded Recipe. The plugin compares the selection's fingerprint with DYE2's fingerprint of the Workflow's profile, DYE2's own test of a profile change; a selection with no fingerprint matches. A selection made on another profile, as one DYE2 restored after the profile changed, loads nothing, and DYE2 clears it at the Workflow's next change. A favourite selected, or nothing, loads none.

The plugin reads the selection from DYE2's endpoint on each push from DYE2's `changed` socket, about 2 s after each change of the Workflow (past DYE2's 1.5 s wait before it records), which decides on the changes it holds back meanwhile, and on its poll. A 404 tells nothing: it neither unloads the Recipe nor holds back a write, as a DYE2 not loaded records nothing. But the Machine has no Loaded Recipe where `GET /api/v1/plugins` lists no DYE2, or lists it neither loaded nor to load at startup (`loaded` and `autoLoad` both false, as disabling it leaves it), at once; or lists it not loaded for longer than twice the plugin's poll interval, as a DYE2 that fails to load or one unloaded in Decaid's settings. A loaded DYE2 is available whatever its `autoLoad`. As the Recipe is dropped so, the plugin drops any select it was to try again, keeps any clear, and stores `{}` as DYE2's selection once, so DYE2 coming back restores none over a Bean switch made meanwhile. Nor does a first answer of no selection after DYE2 loads (the plugin's first read, or its first after a 404) unload the Recipe, as DYE2 restores its stored selection only after its load: the plugin takes no selection once a later push or read confirms it, at the latest at its poll. On every read and every change of the Workflow, the plugin keeps the Workflow's context naming the Loaded Recipe, or none (`decentSyncRecipeId`), so Decaid copies it onto every Shot pulled with it. The first Workflow the plugin sees as it loads clears nothing: a barista may have selected something since.

On a load, the plugin writes into the Workflow what applying a DYE2 recipe leaves out, or a stale copy got wrong (DYE2 at `2cf631c` applies some of these itself, as the interim list below says): the Recipe's current dial, its brew temperature, its Bean Batch, grinder, Basket and RPM, and all its hot water settings (ADR-0014). A brew temperature is written as Streamline's Brew tile writes one (`shiftedTemperatures` in `streamline-js:src/modules/brew-temp.js`): the profile's first step goes to it, and every other step moves by the same amount, from the Recipe's Profile's own step temperatures. A profile with no usable first step temperature is written none. Into each recipe record, the server writes the Recipe's profile already at its brew temperature, by the same rule, in DYE2's `profileSnapshot` and the embedded Workflow, and `decentSyncRecipeId` in that Workflow's context. So DYE2's and Streamline's own applies set the brew temperature, and a recipe applied from Streamline's strip names its Recipe, at once.

A write of the plugin's that changes the Workflow's profile, as a load from a stale copy or an edit of the Recipe's Profile does, clears DYE2's selection as a profile change. So the plugin selects the Recipe's slot again right after it, as DYE2's dashboard does after an apply, where DYE2 answers no selection or still that slot, so a selection the barista made since stands. Until the selection is made, its reads neither unload the Recipe nor write `{}`, and a select or clear that fails, or that DYE2 answers 404, is tried again once DYE2 answers. Selecting cancels a record DYE2 had pending, so a brew temperature changed in the 1.5 s before it is not recorded. And before any write into the Workflow while a Recipe is loaded, a load's included, the plugin checks DYE2 still has its slot selected, or nothing while the plugin is still to select it again after a write of its own, so a write only rarely lands in a recipe a barista selected in the moment between.

## Following the Recipe both ways

While a Recipe is loaded, the Machine follows it both ways.

- **From the Machine.** DYE2 records a change of the dose, yield, grind setting, brew temperature, or the hot water value of the recipe's active mode (its volume where it names no mode) into the recipe's record, and the plugin takes it in as the tablet's edit of the Recipe, as it takes in any change of a recipe record (ADR-0005, ADR-0020). For these, DYE2 is the only recorder. A yield set on Streamline's Drink tile is one of them: Streamline sends it as the profile's target weight too, which DYE2 does not count as a change of profile (decentespresso/dye2#18), and with the Workflow's own profile, so its brew temperature is the Workflow's (decentespresso/streamline-js#155), but for a profile with a step limiter of 0 (an accepted gap, below). DYE2 at `2cf631c` records the RPM and Basket too, where the record holds one, which the plugin still also reads from the Workflow below, the same value either way. As DYE2 records only a field the record holds, the server makes every Recipe's record hold a brew temperature, taken from its profile's first step where the Recipe has none. The plugin reads the Workflow itself only for what DYE2 does not record: the RPM, the Basket, every hot water setting but the one DYE2 records (its mode's, or the volume where the record names no mode), and another batch of the Recipe's Bean, as DYE2's recipes name a Bean but no batch (decentespresso/dye2#27). DYE2 records the steam and flush values too, which stay the Location's settings, taken from the Workflow as ADR-0014 says.
- **Stale values.** The server takes a part set back as stale, whoever records it, and writes the Recipe's value back, where it is either:
  - the Recipe's previous value, which the tablet's record held just before the server's latest write of it (a stale save, ADR-0005); or
  - the value the Workflow held just before a write of the plugin's that Decaid refused, as its hot water write while no machine was connected, until that write lands, which DYE2 then records back from the Workflow left behind its record; or
  - for a part the load write changed, the value the Workflow held just before that write, set back before the first Shot pulled on that Machine after the load. DYE2's dashboard reads the Workflow once, as it opens, and sends that whole copy back with each change made there and on Done, which Decaid merges into the Workflow, so every send would undo what the load wrote of a field the copy holds (an interim workaround, below). The parts DYE2's or Streamline's apply sets, the dose, yield, grind setting, RPM, grinder (where DYE2's page lists it) and the active hot water value where the recipe names them, and the Basket on Streamline's, hold the Recipe's values already as the load is written, so the load write leaves them alone: a later change of one back is the barista's own. So the rule falls on what the apply left out: the batch with its coffee, the Basket on DYE2's, the other hot water settings, and whatever a stale copy left out. The grinder is never stale this way: a grinder set back is a change of grinder, as dye2#19 has it, even where DYE2's page lacked the Recipe's grinder in the list it read as it opened (an accepted gap, below).

    The first Shot is the first espresso the Machine starts after the load (Decaid keeps no Shot for one stopped before its pour). The server takes the first such change of state delivered after the Workflow that loaded the Recipe, in delivery order, not by its time, and not by the Shot's own delivery, which can come later. The plugin delivers a change of state after any Workflow change reported before it, and after any reported while the plugin holds the Workflow's changes back for its own write, so a dashboard send just before an espresso still counts as before it. A change of state held back when the plugin stops is lost, and the window stays open until the next espresso.

  For a field DYE2 records, the server compares the value DYE2 recorded with what the Workflow held before the plugin's write. A batch set back so is stale, not a change of Bean, and unloads nothing: the plugin, which decides unloads, skips one set back to what its load write replaced, before the first Shot. A part set back over the reporting tablet's own latest edit of it is that barista undoing their tweak, and is an edit, as #176 has it. The cost: a barista cannot set one of these parts back to exactly what the load replaced until they have pulled a Shot. DYE2 at `2cf631c` clears its selection on any change of batch, so one who switches back to the pre-load batch meanwhile unloads the Recipe all the same (#188). Any other batch of another Bean, or any other grinder, is a change of Bean or grinder, and unloads the Recipe.
- **To other Machines.** An edit of the Recipe from anywhere is written into its record on each tablet at its Location, then into the Workflow of every Machine that has it loaded, the brew temperature as on a load. DYE2 there finds its record holding the values, and records nothing.
- **Kept in the Workflow.** While DYE2 has a recipe selected, Streamline's clear after each Shot leaves the batch, coffee, grinder, Basket and RPM in place, and clears only the barista, drinker and note (`clearDyeWorkflowContext` in `streamline-js:src/modules/dyeStrip.js`, decentespresso/streamline-js#149), so the next Shot records the Recipe's: a Recipe made for a particular Basket is pulled with it on every Machine. Where DYE2 is not loaded or has no recipe selected, Streamline still clears them, or those DYE2 flagged, so they do not label the next Shot. A part cleared is neither an edit nor an unload, and nothing writes it back (an accepted gap, below).
- **Unloading.** When DYE2's selection clears or names something else, the Recipe is unloaded, and the Workflow names none. The plugin clears DYE2's selection (`POST /selection {}`, the call Streamline makes when its DYE strip is turned off) where it still names the slot of a Recipe that has left it: taken out of its slot, Archived, or of a Location the Machine left. A failed clear is tried again, unless DYE2 has selected something since.
- **Capture-only.** As a Machine is made capture-only, the Recipe the server last had as its Loaded Recipe is unloaded and cleared from DYE2's selection, `{}` stored with it. One loaded on the tablet before the server heard of it, as while offline, or selected there afterwards, which nothing there shares, stays selected and is recorded into there, but is not its Loaded Recipe; once its sharing is turned back on, it is, where its slot holds one of its Location's Recipes. A Capture-only Machine is written nothing else, so none of the workarounds below runs on it.
- **Slots.** Moving the Recipe to another slot keeps it loaded: the plugin clears the selection before rewriting the list, and selects the new slot after, and its reads meanwhile do not unload the Recipe. So DYE2 never records into a recipe put in the old slot meanwhile. Likewise, before the plugin's write removes the recipe in the slot DYE2 has selected, or replaces it with another Recipe's, as the server removes a joining Machine's own recipes or another Location's Recipes and writes its new Location's in their slots (ADR-0008), it clears the selection and stores `{}`, whether or not that recipe is the Loaded Recipe: DYE2 keeps its selection through its recipe's removal, and would record into the recipe put in that slot, which the plugin would then take as loaded.

A skin built for a Decent Sync deployment applies a recipe as Streamline does: it writes the Workflow, then selects the recipe in DYE2.

## Interim: workarounds and accepted gaps

Each workaround here does on DYE2's or Streamline's behalf what an upstream ask would have them do, and goes when they do (AGENTS.md). Several of those asks are answered on the supported floor already (ADR-0017); each such workaround goes with the ticket named:

- **DYE2's dashboard copy.** The rule that a part the load write changed is stale when set back to its pre-load value before the first Shot, with the plugin holding back changes of state while it holds the Workflow's, stands in for DYE2's dashboard reading the Workflow again before each send (decentespresso/dye2#31). The rule for a write Decaid refused stays.
- **A change of grinder or Bean.** The plugin unloads the Recipe and clears DYE2's selection itself on a change of grinder, or of batch to one of another Bean (decentespresso/dye2#19). DYE2 at `2cf631c` clears its selection itself on any change of batch or grinder, so its clear comes first, and #188 removes the plugin's. A grinder or batch cleared is no such change for the plugin, nor is a batch set back to what the load replaced, before the first Shot (Stale values, above).
- **The Recipe's id in the Workflow.** The plugin writes `decentSyncRecipeId`, and the server reads it (decentespresso/dye2#20). DYE2 at `2cf631c` writes its selection into the Workflow itself, which #189 reads instead. Until then, a Shot started between a recipe applied from DYE2's dashboard and the plugin's write carries the Recipe loaded before it, or none.
- **The RPM, Basket and batch** are read from the Workflow (decentespresso/dye2#21, and decentespresso/dye2#27 for the batch). DYE2 at `2cf631c` records the RPM and Basket, and holds and applies a recipe's batch, which #187 follows.
- **The brew temperature** is written on a load, and into each record's profile (decentespresso/dye2#26). DYE2 at `2cf631c` applies a recipe's own, which #187 follows.
- **Reading after each Workflow change** decides on the changes the plugin holds back, and stood in for DYE2 pushing a cleared selection, which DYE2 at `2cf631c` does (decentespresso/dye2#24). #189 removes it where the hold does not need it.
- **A cleared selection kept cleared.** When DYE2's endpoint answers no selection after naming a recipe, or the plugin clears it itself, or once as DYE2 becomes unavailable while a Recipe is loaded, the plugin writes `{}` to DYE2's store key `selection` (`POST /api/v1/store/dye2.reaplugin/selection`), which DYE2 restores as no selection. A slot move's own clear writes it too, as the server moves a Recipe by removing its record before creating the new one, and the new slot's selection is stored as the plugin makes it; nothing is written there by a no selection read while a select of the plugin's is pending. It reads the endpoint again after, and should DYE2 have selected something meanwhile, writes that instead. DYE2 at `2cf631c` stores `{}` itself on every clear (decentespresso/dye2#22), so this now covers only a Bean or grinder switched while DYE2 was away, which DYE2 would restore its selection over; #188 covers that by the selection's own batch and grinder instead.

Until #187 and #188, a gap is transitional. DYE2 at `2cf631c` clears its selection when the Workflow's batch or grinder moves off the one it held as the recipe was applied, or the first it saw since, where it held none. So where a write of the plugin's or the server's moves it, the Recipe unloads:

- Streamline's strip applies the record's context and profile, whose embedded context holds no batch until #187, and leaves the Workflow's batch as it was. On a Machine whose Workflow holds another batch, the load write's batch unloads the Recipe.
- A Loaded Recipe write of an edit of its batch or grinder, made on another Machine or in the management interface, unloads it on each Machine that has it loaded.
- DYE2's dashboard apply clears the Workflow's batch where the recipe names a Bean and no batch, as every record the server writes does until #187, so the load write's batch is the first DYE2 sees, and stands. But the dashboard's copy, still holding none, sends the batch cleared with its next change made there or on Done, which unloads the Recipe (decentespresso/dye2#31).

And these gaps are accepted until fixed upstream:

- DYE2's dashboard sending back on Done a Workflow whose profile is not the Loaded Recipe's, as from a recipe list read before its Profile was edited, changes the profile, so DYE2 clears its selection and the Recipe unloads (decentespresso/dye2#31).
- DYE2's dashboard page whose grinder list, read as it opened, lacks the Recipe's grinder leaves the grinder out of its apply, so its copy sends the previous grinder back, which unloads the Recipe (decentespresso/dye2#31).
- DYE2's dashboard left open through a Shot sends its copy with each later change made there and on Done, past the first Shot, so its pre-load batch, Basket and hot water settings become the Recipe's edits, or a batch of another Bean unloads it (decentespresso/dye2#31).
- The plugin reads DYE2's selection before it posts a list emptying or refilling a slot, and DYE2 has no conditional select, so a barista selecting that slot in the moment between keeps the selection, of the recipe put there. A clear DYE2 answered 404 to is made at the plugin's next read while DYE2 has the slot selected, so a barista who selects the recipe now in that slot first, as DYE2 comes back, has it cleared once.
- DYE2's recording reaches the server only with the tablet's recipes, on the plugin's poll of its collections, so a part the load write changed that DYE2 records, set back before the first espresso but reported after it, is taken as an edit. DYE2 at `2cf631c` records the Basket, RPM and every hot water setting too, so this falls on a dashboard copy's set-back of any part the load write changed but the batch. #189 closes this by reporting DYE2's recipes on each DYE2 push.
- When Streamline's post of its selection fails, it tries it again, but DYE2 meanwhile records the apply's own Workflow change into the item it had selected before, where that change leaves it selected (decentespresso/streamline-js#148, #186).
- Streamline sends a step limiter of value 0 as none in every profile it sends (`updateWorkflow` in `streamline-js:src/modules/api.js`), and DYE2's fingerprint counts that as a change of profile. So on a profile with such a step, as Decaid's bundled Adaptive v3, a yield or dose set on Streamline's tiles clears DYE2's selection, and the Recipe unloads, where the Workflow's profile was last written whole by another than Streamline, as by DYE2's dashboard apply or the plugin's load write. A Recipe applied from Streamline's strip, which sends its profile the same way, keeps its selection through them. No upstream ask is filed for this yet.
- A Shot stored while DYE2 is not loaded, or has no recipe selected, has Streamline clear the batch, coffee, grinder, Basket and RPM from the Workflow, or those DYE2 flagged, so the Shots after it carry none of them until a load or a Loaded Recipe write sets them again, even where the Recipe stays loaded, as through a DYE2 reload.

## Rejected

We first worked the Loaded Recipe out by matching the Workflow against the Location's Recipes, on profile, dose, yield, grinder and grind setting, and read every Workflow change while one was loaded as an edit of it. We rejected keeping that once DYE2 selected and recorded recipes itself. The two would be separate models of the same thing, which disagree: DYE2 would keep recording into a recipe the plugin had unloaded, or into another than the one it matched, and the server would send that recipe's changes to every Machine. And recording ourselves the fields DYE2 records would make two recorders of one change, which count it twice or disagree about it. We follow the apps' own model and ask upstream for what it lacks.

We rejected sharing only the stored recipes, picked up the next time a barista loads one, because Shots would record a dial the Machine no longer has. We rejected leaving the Recipe off the Workflow until DYE2 records it there, because Shots pulled while the tablet is offline would then reach the server without their Recipe.

## Consequences

- **Every tweak is the Recipe's.** A change on a Machine with a Recipe loaded to its dose, yield, grind setting, brew temperature, RPM, Basket, hot water, or batch of the Recipe's Bean changes it on every Machine at the Location. That is the intent: a dose is never changed for one customer.
- **Only an applied recipe is loaded.** Dialling in a Recipe's values by hand loads nothing. Applying a favourite while a Recipe is loaded unloads it, as DYE2 then selects the favourite.
- **DYE2 is required.** A Machine without DYE2 `main` at `2cf631c` or later has no Loaded Recipe (ADR-0017). Nothing falls back to matching. The plugin still writes the recipe lists to every sharing tablet (ADR-0005).
- **A change DYE2 skips is not the Recipe's.** DYE2 records only fields the recipe holds, so a change to one a Recipe leaves blank, but the brew temperature, stays on that Machine.
- **The plugin writes more of the tablet.** Beyond milestone 2's shared settings and the clearing on joining (ADR-0008), it writes a Loaded Recipe's values into the Workflow, DYE2's selection and its stored selection, and each recipe record's profile at the Recipe's brew temperature. A skin that remembers the hot water settings takes them as its own (ADR-0021).
- **Shots name their Recipe, and record its Basket.** Each Shot carries the Recipe its Machine had loaded, and the Recipe's batch, grinder, Basket and RPM. A Shot is linked to its Recipe by its Machine's Loaded Recipes when it was pulled, not by the id alone: a Capture-only Machine's Shots name a recipe that was never its Loaded Recipe. So milestone 5 needs no inferred recipes.
