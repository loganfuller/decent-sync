# Library items have per-location state

A library item's content (a profile's steps, a batch's roast date) is the same everywhere. Some of its state differs by location: whether a profile, basket or tool is shown there, whether a batch is at that location, and the batch's stock there. A bean is offered at a location while one of its batches is there, and, until one of its batches is added there, where a tablet there created it, linked a bean of its own to it (ADR-0018) or un-archived it. The server keeps this state per location.

A tablet holds only what its machine's location offers. An item is written to it when the location starts offering it, and hidden or archived on it, never deleted, when the location stops, so the tablet's shots still find it. DYE2's records can't be hidden or archived, so they are removed from its lists instead, and shots keep the names DYE2 stored on them (ADR-0005). On a tablet, a batch's `archived` flag is true when the batch isn't at that location, and its `weightRemaining` is that location's stock. Editing those fields on a tablet changes them only for that location: un-archiving a batch adds it there, archiving it finishes it there, and a corrected `weightRemaining` is recorded as a count there.

This is surprising because Decaid has a single `archived` flag and a single `weightRemaining` per record, which reads as global. Decaid has no notion of location, so the plugin maps the fields per tablet.

New profiles, baskets and tools are shown only at the location where they were created and hidden elsewhere until someone shows them there. That keeps lab experiments out of the cafes' lists. Decaid's bundled profiles aren't synced, but whether they are shown is per location like any other profile. New batches follow the same rule: a batch is at the location where it was created, and at another location only once someone adds it there, in the management interface or on a tablet there that still holds it. Because a bean follows its batches, a lab's sample coffees stay at the lab too. A lab therefore needs no special treatment: everything new starts where it was made, and reaches another location only when someone puts it there.

## Consequences

- **A batch's presence is explicit, not derived from stock.** A batch is at a location from when it's added there until it's finished there, so an owner who never enters weights still has batches where they use them. Before Stock (milestone 6), a location's stock of a batch is the latest `weightRemaining` entered there, last writer winning, which milestone 6 reads as a count. Stock Movements then become further ways to add and finish a batch, such as a delivery adding it and a count of zero finishing it.
- **Promoting an item to another location happens in the management interface.** A cafe's tablets don't hold the lab's profiles or batches, so they can't show or add one there; someone does it in the management interface, which every tablet at that location then receives.
- **When a machine joins a location, the location's state wins.** A machine joins a location when it is adopted there, moved there, or has sharing turned back on after being a Capture-only Machine. Its tablet is written what the location offers: its shown profiles, baskets and tools, the batches at the location and their beans, the location's grinders, and its Recipes, in their Recipe Slots or none. What belongs only to the previous location is hidden or archived on it. Its Workflow's grinder, batch and basket are cleared if the location doesn't offer them, and so is its Loaded Recipe (ADR-0022), while its profile, dose, yield, and steam, hot water and rinse settings stay: clearing them is an exception to a Workflow being the machine's own. Nothing its tablet changed or added before it joined is taken into the library: its items the library lacks are archived or hidden on it, unless the location offers no item of that kind yet, which it then brings (ADR-0018). A machine that leaves every location keeps what its tablet has and becomes a Capture-only Machine. Recipes follow the same rule: the previous location's are removed from DYE2's list and the location's written under their record ids, each in its slot or none, and a joining machine's own recipes, brought only to a location with no Recipes, keep their ids and slots (ADR-0005, ADR-0018).
- **Capture-only Machines take no part.** Nothing of the Library is written to a Capture-only Machine's tablet (Settings Edits still reach it, ADR-0023), and what is added or changed there isn't taken into the library, then or when it joins a location, so an Admin can turn sharing off to experiment on a machine without changing its location's library.
