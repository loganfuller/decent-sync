# Library items have per-location state

A library item's content (a profile's steps, a batch's roast date) is the same everywhere. Some of its state differs by location: whether a profile is shown there, whether a batch is at that location, and the batch's stock there. A bean is offered at a location while one of its batches is there, and, until one of its batches is added there, where a tablet there created it, linked a bean of its own to it (ADR-0018) or un-archived it. The server keeps this state per location.

A tablet holds only what its machine's location offers. An item is written to it when the location starts offering it, and hidden or archived on it, never deleted, when the location stops, so the tablet's shots still find it. On a tablet, a batch's `archived` flag is true when the batch isn't at that location, and its `weightRemaining` is that location's stock. Editing those fields on a tablet changes them only for that location: un-archiving a batch adds it there, archiving it finishes it there, and a corrected `weightRemaining` is recorded as a count there.

This is surprising because Decaid has a single `archived` flag and a single `weightRemaining` per record, which reads as global. Decaid has no notion of location, so the plugin maps the fields per tablet.

New profiles are shown only at the location where they were created and hidden elsewhere until someone shows them there. That keeps lab experiments out of the cafes' lists. Decaid's bundled profiles aren't synced, but whether they are shown is per location like any other profile. New batches follow the same rule: a batch is at the location where it was created, and at another location only once someone adds it there, in the management interface or on a tablet there that still holds it. Because a bean follows its batches, a lab's sample coffees stay at the lab too. A lab therefore needs no special treatment: everything new starts where it was made, and reaches another location only when someone puts it there.

## Consequences

- **A batch's presence is explicit, not derived from stock.** A batch is at a location from when it's added there until it's finished there, so an owner who never enters weights still has batches where they use them. Before Stock (milestone 4), a location's stock of a batch is the latest `weightRemaining` entered there, last writer winning, which milestone 4 reads as a count. Stock Movements then become further ways to add and finish a batch, such as a delivery adding it and a count of zero finishing it.
- **Promoting an item to another location happens in the management interface.** A cafe's tablets don't hold the lab's profiles or batches, so they can't show or add one there; someone does it in the management interface, which every tablet at that location then receives.
- **When a machine joins a location, the location's state wins.** A machine joins a location when it is adopted there, moved there, or has sharing turned back on after being a Capture-only Machine. Its tablet is written what the location offers: its shown profiles, the batches at the location and their beans, the location's grinders, and the location's steam, hot water and rinse settings, unless its sharing of them is turned off (ADR-0014). What belongs only to the previous location is hidden or archived on it. Its Workflow's grinder and batch are cleared if the location doesn't offer them, while its profile, dose and yield stay (an exception to ADR-0014's rule that a Workflow is the machine's own). A machine joining a location with no steam, hot water and rinse settings yet brings its own. A machine that leaves every location keeps what its tablet has and becomes a Capture-only Machine. ADR-0005 applies the same rule to recipe slots.
- **Capture-only Machines take no part.** Nothing is written to a Capture-only Machine's tablet, and what is added or changed there isn't taken into the library. Its items join the library when it joins a location (ADR-0018).
