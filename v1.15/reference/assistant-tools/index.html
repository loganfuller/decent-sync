Assistant tools

An assistant works with Decent Sync through the tools on this page, 58 in all: 25 that read and 33 that change something. They are what the server’s MCP endpoint offers a Connected App. Assistants covers connecting one and what it may do as you, and On the internet covers what the server needs and how to turn assistants off.

This page is written from the server’s own tool definitions each time the site is built, so it lists exactly the tools of the release it documents. Each description is the one the server gives the assistant, word for word. It is written for the assistant to read, so it names the fields of its answers and other tools as the assistant sees them. The words in capitals are defined in the Glossary.

Every tool acts as the person who approved the app, within what their account may do. Where a description ends with Admins only, the tool refuses anyone else’s app; where it says so of one setting or field, only that part is kept to Admins. Every answer is kept small, so a list comes a page at a time; How much an answer holds has how small, and which apps take less.

Tools that read

Any Connected App may call these, whether it was approved to read only or to read and change, and each answers with what its account may see: a Viewer’s app is refused the Drafts, and only an Admin’s is given the names of the accounts behind recorded changes.

list_locations
List Locations		Lists every Location, by name, with its IANA time zone, in which a day at the Location is counted, and the Machines at it now.
find_library_items
Find Library items		Finds the Library's items of one kind, a page at a time, as the management interface lists them, by Location, name and whether Archived, and a Bean Batch by its Batch Code: Beans, Bean Batches, Profiles, Grinders, Baskets, Tools or Recipes. Recipes come by Location, in Recipe Slot order, then those in no slot, each with its Reference Shot. list_location_batches lists one Location's Bean Batches with the Recipes there that name each. An item's content is its record's fields, as the tablet that created it sent them and as edited since, Decaid's names for a Bean's, Bean Batch's, Grinder's and Profile's, and DYE2's for a Basket's and Tool's. A Grinder's also holds, under DYE2's names, its feedRate note and the range and default of its setting and of its RPM (settingMin, settingMax, settingDefault, rpmMin, rpmMax, rpmDefault), where 0 is a value: a tablet's steppers stop at the range, and a Machine takes the default, or the middle of the range, as a barista picks the Grinder on it. A Recipe's content is in the Library's terms: dose and yield in grams, brewTemperature in °C, grindSetting as typed, and any steam, hot water and flush settings in °C, seconds, ml/s and ml. A Bean Batch's remainingWeight at a Location is its Remaining Weight there, in grams, worked out as it is read: the weight its count there starts from (entered), which is the weight last entered there or the amount a Batch Move brought where it had none, less the dose of each Shot linked to the batch that was pulled there since (used), plus what Batch Moves have brought there since and less what they have taken away (moved), never below 0. One with no weight at a Location is untracked there: its remainingWeight there is null, not 0, and Shots take nothing from it. A Batch Move takes a batch from one Location it is at to another, all of it or part of its weight, and never changes the batch's total. A Profile marked bundled is one of Decaid's own, which every tablet has. A Bean's likelyDuplicates are other Beans with the same roaster and name, ignoring case, never merged. Each page gives total, offset and more before its list: more says whether more match after it, as a page may hold fewer than limit to keep the response small, and the next page's offset is this one's plus its length. Times are ISO 8601 with a Location's offset: an item's createdAt with that of the Location it joined the Library at; a time of its state at a Location, such as a Bean Batch added there, and each edit of that state, applied or lost, with that Location's; and a Grinder's or Recipe's other times and edits with that of the Location it belongs to, but a Recipe's Reference Shot's pulledAt, with that of the Location it was pulled at. Others, such as a Bean's content edits, are in UTC.

get_profile_versions
Get a Profile's versions		Reads a Profile's Profile Versions, as its page in the management interface lists them: each Profile in the chain of Decaid's parentId links through it, as a tablet saves a Profile whose steps changed as a new one saved from the old, with when and by whom each was saved, where each is shown and how many Shots are on each; and the Recipes that name the Profile asked about, with the Machines that have each loaded now. get_library_item reads each version's steps, and simulate_profile runs each on the same puck to compare them. list_location_batches
List a Location's Bean Batches		Lists the Bean Batches at one Location, a page at a time, as the Location's page in the management interface lists them: those there now, none finished there or moved away from it entirely, Archived or of an Archived Bean, the latest roasted first. Each has its Bean, roast date and Batch Code; its Remaining Weight there (remainingWeight), null where it is untracked there, which is not 0; the weight its count there starts from (entered); the Shots pulled there since and the coffee they used (used); what Batch Moves have brought there and taken away since (moved); and the Recipes at the Location that name it, with each one's Recipe Slot. Every number is that Location's own: a batch at two Locations is in each one's list with that Location's weight, Shots and moves. find_shots with beanBatchId and locationId lists a batch's Shots there, get_library_item reads a batch at every Location it is at, with every Recipe that names it, and find_library_items lists batches across Locations. Each page gives total, offset and more before its list: more says whether more match after it, as a page may hold fewer than limit to keep the response small, and the next page's offset is this one's plus its length. Times are ISO 8601 with the Location's offset.
list_machines
List Machines		Lists every Machine, by name, with its model, such as DE1PRO or Bengle: where it is now; online, whether its tablet's plugin is connected now, and lastSeenAt, its plugin's latest heartbeat or report; its last Machine State, as Decaid names its state and substate, such as espresso and pouring, or sleeping; its Loaded Recipe, the Recipe a barista last applied on it, with its Recipe Slot, 1 to 9, and unloadedAt null while still loaded; lastShot, the Shot it pulled last, with its Recipe, its duration in seconds and its spec, as get_shot gives it: outOfSpec is a duration more than 10% from its Recipe's Reference Shot's, or a yield more than 5% from its target, and stopped is how it stopped, targetWeight and targetVolume being stopped at target, Decaid stopping it as it reached its target weight on the scale (targetWeight) or, with no scale, its target volume (targetVolume); and captureOnly, why it is a Capture-only Machine, whose records are kept but which takes no part in the Library: noLocation, sharingOff, or empty while it takes part. Times are ISO 8601 with the offset of the time zone of the Location the Machine was at then, by its Location History, or UTC's while it was at none; a Machine State's and a Loaded Recipe's times, and its Workflow's observedAt, are by its tablet's clock.
get_machine
Get a Machine		One Machine: its Workflow, Machine Settings, its tablet's Tablet Settings, its Location History, its sharing status, and the versions on its tablet, besides what list_machines gives: where it is now; online, whether its tablet's plugin is connected now, and lastSeenAt, its plugin's latest heartbeat or report; its last Machine State, as Decaid names its state and substate, such as espresso and pouring, or sleeping; its Loaded Recipe, the Recipe a barista last applied on it, with its Recipe Slot, 1 to 9, and unloadedAt null while still loaded; lastShot, with its Recipe, duration and spec, as list_machines describes: outOfSpec is beyond 10% of its Recipe's Reference Shot's duration or 5% of its target yield, and targetWeight and targetVolume are stopped at target; and captureOnly, why it is a Capture-only Machine, whose records are kept but which takes no part in the Library: noLocation, sharingOff, or empty while it takes part. Its Workflow, what it is set up to do next, with who made its latest change, is summarised; raw adds it whole, to hundredths. Its summary holds the parts the management interface shows, by the Workflow's names: profile, the Profile's title; brewTemperature °C, its first step's; dose and yield g; grinder, its model; recipe, the one its context names, by the Library's name; steam (°C, s, ml/s), hotWater (°C, ml) and flush (°C, s). profileId, beanBatchId, grinderId, basketId and recipeId are the Library's ids of what it names, for get_library_item as profile, beanBatch, grinder, basket and recipe: null where the Library holds none, as for a batch not yet in it, or a change received before Decent Sync linked them. cause says who made a Workflow change: plugin, one of Decent Sync's own writes, which pluginWrite names; other, anyone else, such as a barista on Streamline, Streamline itself or DYE2, which can't be told apart; unknown, for one from before Decent Sync recorded this, or from an older plugin. pluginWrite is load, the Recipe the Workflow came to name loaded; loadedRecipe, an edit of the Loaded Recipe made elsewhere written into it; clear, the Recipe unloaded, or a grinder, batch or Basket the Location doesn't offer cleared; steamOn, steam turned back on at the Loaded Recipe's steam temperature and time; or draft, a Draft it follows written. Its Machine Settings (machineSettings, advancedSettings, machineCalibration, its flow calibration, and sensorCalibration) and its tablet's Tablet Settings (appSettings, and presenceSettings, when it sleeps and wakes) are each as Decaid sent it, by Decaid's field names and in its units, as edit_machine_settings lists them, sleepTimeoutMinutes and a wake schedule's keepAwakeFor in minutes, kept while later reports are unavailable (available false), with utcOffsetMinutes, the tablet's offset, for a value whose times have none; null for one never reported. With at, its Machine Settings and Tablet Settings are each as last received at or before then, by its Settings History, available true and reportedAt its receivedAt, when that value was received; null for one none was received of by then, or none kept: a Settings History begins with the value held as it was first kept, dated when that value was last reported, so earlier times give null though the Machine had settings. A change is received about 30 to 60 s after it was made, later while the Machine was busy, and within a day for the sensor calibration, and what a Settings History compares none of, such as the tablet's battery, may have changed since without being kept. Everything else, its Workflow included, is as now: get_machine_activity gives its Workflow then. Its sharing status gives how many Library changes its tablet is due (waiting, null while it is written nothing), the last it applied, and those it refused, with Decaid's answer. Times are ISO 8601 with the offset of the time zone of the Location the Machine was at then, by its Location History, or UTC's while it was at none; a Machine State's and a Loaded Recipe's times, and its Workflow's observedAt, are by its tablet's clock.
get_machine_reports
Get what a Machine's tablet reports		What one Machine's tablet last reported besides its settings and its Library: its scale, sensors, paired devices, plugins and Skins, each collection asked for, all five if none. scaleInfo is the connected scale's own report, such as its firmwareVersion and batteryLevel, which only some scales give, unavailable while none is connected; sensors, the sensors Decaid lists as connected, each by its id with its manifest (info): its name, vendor, data and commands; plugins, Decaid's plugin list, each plugin's manifest, such as its id, name and version, with loaded, autoLoad, source, how it was installed, such as {"kind":"github_branch","branch":"dist"}, or null for a bundled one, and pendingUpdate; skins, Decaid's Skins as {skins, default, server}: those installed, each with its id, name, version and path, the default Skin, null with none installed, and the WebUI server's status, whose path is the Skin it serves, null while it serves nothing. Each of these four is as Decaid sent it, by Decaid's names, its numbers to hundredths: available false when the latest read had nothing, the value read before kept; reportedAt when the latest report was received, receivedAt when its value was; null for one never reported. pairedDevices are the devices paired with its tablet, leaving out those only discovered nearby, as the management interface's Paired devices card shows them: the Scale that weighs Shots, the one Decaid has connected as primary or else the one it prefers; an Auxiliary Scale; its sensors; and others, such as the machine itself; each with its model as Decaid names it, its connection state when last reported and a sensor's vendor, the connected scale with the firmware and battery level scaleInfo gives, if any; reportedAt, available and receivedAt as for the others, null if never reported. Each is the latest reported: list_settings_history gives each change of them, and get_machine the DYE2 and Streamline among them. Times are ISO 8601 with the offset of the time zone of the Location the Machine was at then, or UTC's while it was at none.
get_machine_activity
Get a Machine's activity		What happened on one Machine over a time range, by its tablet's clock: its Workflow changes as a dial-in trail, its Loaded Recipes and its Machine States, each list latest first. Times are given with the offset of the Location it was at then. Each list counts what was in effect at any time in the range, so its last (oldest) entry, on its last page, may date from the range's start or before: what was in effect as it began. limit and offset page each list, and kinds asks for some of them alone. Each page gives total, offset and more before its list: more says whether more match after it, as a page may hold fewer than limit to keep the response small, and the next page's offset is this one's plus its length. The dial-in trail is one list of its Recipes loaded and unloaded and what changed between them, by kind: inEffect, its last (oldest) entry, the Workflow in effect as the range began, whole (workflow); loaded, a Recipe loaded, with replaced, one unloaded as it loaded; unloaded; recipeUpdated, Decent Sync writing in an edit of the Loaded Recipe made elsewhere, whose own history says who made it; changed, any other. Each gives who made its first change, and loadedRecipe, the Recipe loaded, unloaded or updated, else the one loaded then; each but inEffect gives changes, each field of the summary that changed, before and after, a part's as steam.duration. A load or unload folds in the first change within 5 s after it, which made it, and in those 5 s Decent Sync's load writes, or for an unload changes of that one's cause; one with no change within 5 s, as on a Location join, is of cause unknown, a later change its own entry. Other changes of one cause and pluginWrite within 5 s of each other merge, a group spanning at most 30 s and never a load or unload; unknown ones, from before Decent Sync recorded who made them, fold and merge with each other by time alone. lastObservedAt is the last change an entry took after it began. An entry left with no field changed is dropped, except a load or unload. total counts the entries; cause keeps those of one cause, and inEffect, each before then as the changes left out left it. Its summary holds the parts the management interface shows, by the Workflow's names: profile, the Profile's title; brewTemperature °C, its first step's; dose and yield g; grinder, its model; recipe, the one its context names, by the Library's name; steam (°C, s, ml/s), hotWater (°C, ml) and flush (°C, s). profileId, beanBatchId, grinderId, basketId and recipeId are the Library's ids of what it names, for get_library_item as profile, beanBatch, grinder, basket and recipe: null where the Library holds none, as for a batch not yet in it, or a change received before Decent Sync linked them. cause says who made a Workflow change: plugin, one of Decent Sync's own writes, which pluginWrite names; other, anyone else, such as a barista on Streamline, Streamline itself or DYE2, which can't be told apart; unknown, for one from before Decent Sync recorded this, or from an older plugin. pluginWrite is load, the Recipe the Workflow came to name loaded; loadedRecipe, an edit of the Loaded Recipe made elsewhere written into it; clear, the Recipe unloaded, or a grinder, batch or Basket the Location doesn't offer cleared; steamOn, steam turned back on at the Loaded Recipe's steam temperature and time; or draft, a Draft it follows written. A plugin change may also hold a change someone else made while Decent Sync's write was under way, as a barista turning steam back on is given as steamOn. A Machine State lasts until the next one reported.
get_machine_use
Get a Machine's use		How much one Machine was used over a time range, by day or by hour: how many Shots, Steam Records, flushes and hot water pours it made, and how long it spent in each Machine State. In each day or hour: shots, the Shots credited to it that were pulled then, cleaning and calibration Shots included; steamRecords, its Steam Records recorded then; flushes and hotWaterPours, each one visit to that Machine State, counted in the day or hour it began in, with no weight or volume, which Decaid doesn't record; and states, the seconds it spent in each of Decaid's states, the longest first, by state, as get_machine_activity names it, such as espresso, steam, flush, hotWater, idle or sleeping, whatever its substates, so a steam's purge counts as steam, with name, the Machine State as the management interface names it, such as Pulling a shot or Asleep. A Machine State lasts until the next one reported, by the tablet's clock, cut at each day's or hour's edge and at now. So a Machine whose tablet was off counts as in its last state until its tablet reports again: a Machine put to sleep while its tablet is off reads as idle, or whatever it did last, for those hours. Monitoring, a later milestone, will correct this from each Machine's connection history. Nothing is counted before its first Machine State was reported, so a day's seconds may add up to less than the day. A day or an hour is that of the Location the Machine was at then, by its Location History, in its time zone, or UTC's before the day it came to its first Location, which is that one's, and begins when it began in UTC if that was sooner, those first hours in UTC counted in that date's, or by hour the same hour's, bucket: a day the clocks change in is 23 or 25 hours long, an hour they repeat is one hour two hours long, and on a day the Machine moved between time zones each part of the day goes by where it was, while a date given as from or to is still that day where it was at noon UTC, so ask for the days either side of a move to have all of it. buckets are oldest first, each with its date and, by hour, its hour, 0 to 23, those with nothing counted included, and none after now. by day takes a range of at most 31 days, and with no from gives the 14 days ending with the day the range ends in; by hour takes a range of at most 25 hours, the longest a day is, and with no from gives the day the range ends in. Neither limit counts the first hours in UTC of the day it came to its first Location, as far as the range reaches into that day there, whose buckets they share. from and to give the range read, to no later than now, with the offset of the time zone of the Location the Machine was at then, or UTC's while it was at none.

list_settings_history
List a Machine's Settings History		One Machine's Settings History, newest first: each change of what its tablet reported for its Machine Settings, Tablet Settings, plugins, Skins, paired devices, scale and sensors, whatever made it. collection: machineSettings, advancedSettings, machineCalibration (its flow calibration) and sensorCalibration are its Machine Settings; appSettings, and presenceSettings, when it sleeps and wakes, its tablet's Tablet Settings; plugins and skins, Decaid's plugins and Skins; pluginSettings, the plugins' settings, each field a plugin's id, a slash and the name of a setting that is set, its value shown to Admins only and a secret's never; pairedDevices, the devices paired with its tablet; scaleInfo, its scale's firmware; sensors, its sensors. Each change gives each field changed, before and after, as compared: by Decaid's field names and in its units, as get_machine gives them; a wake schedule as schedules/ and its id, added, changed or removed, with its time, daysOfWeek, enabled and keepAwakeFor in minutes; the sensor calibration by target (flow, pressure, temperature), its measured value; a plugin, paired device or sensor by its id, a paired device without how it is connected now. Never a change, as left out of what is compared: the Workflow's steam, hot water and flush settings and the tank temperature, whose changes get_machine_activity gives as Workflow changes, the USB charger, the tablet's battery, the wake schedules' keep-awake end, the scale's battery, and the update checks Decaid makes of plugins. settingsEdit is the Settings Edit that made the change, by its id in list_settings_edits, with who made it, named to Admins only; null for a change made outside Decent Sync, on the tablet, by another app or by the machine itself, which is tied to no person. A read-back of an edit that also holds changes made outside it gives two changes of one id. receivedAt is when the server received the change: about 30 to 60 s after it was made, later while the Machine was busy, and within a day for the sensor calibration. The first value of each collection, where its history begins, is no change: get_machine with at gives it for the Machine Settings and Tablet Settings. Brief changes, a field changed from outside Decent Sync and changed back within about 2 minutes, such as the refill kit Decaid shows on automatic after the machine reconnects, or a scale left out of the paired devices while it connects, are left out unless includeBrief, and then are one change with backAt, when it changed back. Each page gives total, offset and more before its list: more says whether more match after it, as a page may hold fewer than limit to keep the response small, and the next page's offset is this one's plus its length. Times are ISO 8601 with the offset of the time zone of the Location the Machine was at then, or UTC's while it was at none.
get_machine_software
Get a Machine's Tablet Software		One Machine's Tablet Software, as its tablet last reported it: Decaid, with its version, the update it offers and whether its automatic update checks are on; every plugin, with its version, whether it is enabled, how it updates, any update that waits for approval with the permissions it adds, and the settings it declares; and every Skin, with its version, how it updates, which is the default and which is served. updates.how says what the tablet's next update check does to an item: followsReleases, a newer release of its repository is installed; followsBranch, the head of its branch is; pinned, it stays at its commit, or at its tag where a Skin was installed from the URL of one release's download; followsUrl, a Skin is installed again once its URL serves something new; comesWithDecaid, a Skin Decaid restores from its own repository's latest release each time it starts, so how it updates can't be changed; notUpdated, nothing. While automaticUpdateCheck is false nothing updates by itself. A setting's value is given to Admins only, and a secret's never: only whether it is set. showing is what the tablet shows: the Skin served, the page it reported (its build and since when) where it reports one, whether a newer build is installed than the one showing, and the default Skin where a restart would show another. Of all this an assistant changes only the Skin on screen, with reload_skin: an Admin installs, enables, configures, updates and removes plugins and Skins, changes how each updates, and starts Decaid's update, on the Machine's Software tab in the management interface, as Software Actions, which list_software_actions lists.
list_software_actions

A Draft is a profile being written or changed here, held by Decent Sync alone and offered on no tablet until publish_draft makes it a Profile, though a Machine following it runs its steps. Admins and Staff may read, change, publish and discard any Draft, the later save winning, Staff publishing only to the Locations they work at; a Viewer sees none. Its times are UTC.
