For any DE1 or a Bengle running Decaid

Your Decent espresso machines, working as one.

Enter a bag of beans once and every machine has it. Dial in on one machine, and the others with that recipe follow. Every shot from every machine lands in one place, on a server you host yourself.

Free and open source under the MIT license. In production at its author's cafe.

A Decent tablet keeps everything to itself.

Its shots, its beans, its profiles and its settings. With two machines, you enter every bag of beans twice, dial in twice, and compare shots by walking between tablets. With several cafes, nobody can see across them at all.

Decent Sync makes them one system. Baristas keep using Decaid, DYE2 and Streamline exactly as before. The plugin keeps the tablets in step and sends everything they record to your server.

A Machine is its hardware. Replacing its tablet doesn't make it a new Machine.

Machines are adopted by hand. An Admin adds each one, which issues a token for its plugin.

Machines don't depend on it. If the server goes down, they keep working, and the plugin catches up after.

Running in a few minutes.

You need Docker on a computer your tablets can reach, and Decaid v0.9.3 or newer on each tablet.

Full quick start
curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/loganfuller/decent-sync/main/docker-compose.yml
echo 'PUBLIC_URL=http://192.168.1.20:3000' > .env
docker compose up -d
