The Library is the Beans, Bean Batches, Grinders, Profiles, Baskets, Tools and Recipes that Decent Sync keeps and shares with your Machines. Enter something once, on a tablet or here, and it reaches the other tablets that should have it. This page covers what goes where, where each Bean Batch is and how much of it is left, entering and editing coffee and equipment, Recipes and their Recipe Slots, and what happens to an edit or a delete.

Profiles have a page of their own: [Profiles and Drafts](../profiles-and-drafts/). The terms in capitals are defined in the [Glossary](../../reference/glossary/).

An Admin can change all of the Library. Staff edit its shared content anywhere, and change what belongs to a Location only at the Locations they work at: its Recipes and Grinders, the Bean Batches there, and what it shows. A Viewer sees all of it and changes nothing.

## What is shared with which Machines

Machines share by Location. What a barista enters on one tablet joins the Library and reaches the other tablets at that Location. What reaches another Location depends on the kind of item.

| Item | Which Machines get it |
|---|---|
| Bean | Those at each Location where one of its Bean Batches is. Until a batch is added there, also those where a tablet created the Bean, linked a Bean of its own to it, or restored it. |
| Bean Batch | Those at each Location it is at, each with its remaining weight there. |
| Grinder | Those at the one Location it belongs to. |
| Profile, Basket, Tool | Those at each Location that shows it. |
| Recipe | Those at the one Location it belongs to. |

A Capture-only Machine, one with no Location or with its sharing turned off, takes no part: its Shots are kept, and nothing of the Library is written to its tablet.

Sync never deletes a Bean, Bean Batch, Grinder or Profile from a tablet on its own. What a Location stops offering of these is archived or hidden on its tablets, so their past Shots still find it. The one exception is an Admin deleting an item for good ([Archive or delete](#archive-or-delete)).

DYE2's lists on a tablet, its recipes, baskets and tools, are different, because they hold only what the Location offers now:

- An Archived Recipe, Basket or Tool is removed from them, and so is a Tool the Location hides. A Basket it hides is archived there. Each stays in the Library, and is written back when it is restored or shown again.
- A tablet joining a Location that already has Recipes, not counting Archived ones, drops the recipes of its own that are not the Location's, and the same goes for its baskets and tools. They are not kept. A Location with none of a kind yet takes the joining tablet's in.

## Where a Bean Batch is, and how much is left

A Bean Batch is at a Location from when it is added there until it is finished there. It can be at several at once, each with its own remaining weight.

### Move a Bean Batch to another Location, or finish it there

A batch reaches another Location by a move, of all of it or of part of its weight.

1. Open **Library**, then **Bean Batches**, and open the batch.
2. In its **Where it is** card, press **Move to…** beside the Location it leaves, and choose the Location it joins. Where there is only one you may move it to, it is already chosen.
3. Choose **All of it**, or **Part of it** and type the weight to move, in grams. The dialog says what each Location will hold afterwards, and, for a move of part, that the tablets at both Locations show the new weights.
4. Press the dialog's last button, which says the move it makes, such as "Move 500 g to Riverside".

- Moving part lowers the weight at the Location it leaves by the amount and raises the weight at the Location it joins by the same amount, so a move never changes the batch's total. The amount must be less than what the Location it leaves has. To move everything, choose **All of it**, and if the number there is wrong, enter the corrected weight first.
- Moving all takes everything left and finishes the batch at the Location it left, as **Finish here** does: it is archived on that Location's tablets, and Recipes there go on naming it until someone picks another batch for them. The dialog names those Recipes.
- At the Location it joins, a batch that was not there is added, and the amount arriving is where its count starts. A weight already there rises by the amount.
- A batch with no weight entered at the Location it leaves has none to split, so the choice is **All of it** or **Some of it**, and no weight changes anywhere. To move a known amount, enter its weight there first.
- Each move is one line of the batch's history, such as "Moved 5700 g from Roastery Lab to Belmont", with who made it. There is no undo: a wrong move is corrected by moving back.
- A Skin on a tablet that scans QR codes may bring a batch to its Machine's Location too, when a barista scans the label on a bucket that has just arrived. A batch at one other Location is moved from there, all of it, with its weight. One at several other Locations, or at none, is added with no weight moved, as nothing says which Location's coffee arrived: enter its weight there afterwards. The line in the batch's history names the Machine it was scanned on and the barista named on that Machine, such as "Bar Left, by Sam". A scan creates no batch and brings back no Archived one.

When a batch runs out at a Location, press **Finish here**. A confirmation names the batch and says what finishing does, and you confirm with its last button, which is named for the Location, such as "Finish at Downtown". **Cancel** changes nothing.

- The batch is archived on that Location's tablets and stays in the Library. The weight there goes with it, and no other Location's weight changes.
- Recipes at that Location that name the batch go on naming it, and those in a Recipe Slot read "Batch finished here". The confirmation says how many there are.
- There is no undo. A batch still at another Location comes back by a move from there, and one that is at no Location by **Add it at**. To send what is left somewhere else, cancel and use **Move to…** instead.

Under the Locations the batch is at, the card has a line for each Location it has left, such as "Moved to Riverside 26 Nov" where a move of all took it there, and "Finished 26 Nov" otherwise.

A batch that is at no Location has **Add it at** in its **Where it is** card: choose a Location and press **Add**.

### Track how much of a batch is left

A batch's remaining weight at a Location keeps itself. It is the weight last entered there, less the dose of each Shot pulled there with the batch since, plus what has been moved there since and less what has been moved away. Nobody has to subtract anything, and a number that drifts is corrected by weighing the bucket.

1. Open the batch, and in its **Where it is** card press **Enter weight** beside the Location. Or open the Location's page, and press **Enter weight** in the batch's row of its **Bean Batches** card.
2. Type what is left there, in grams. Where the weight you type differs from the one worked out, the dialog says by how much, such as "140 g less than the 2920 g worked out.", so that 292 typed for 2920 is noticed. It never stops you saving.
3. Press **Save**.

The **Where it is** card leads with **Remaining in all**, which adds up the Locations that track the batch, over a line such as "At 3 Locations, of 6000 g roasted." Where not every Location tracks the batch, the line says how many do, such as "At 3 Locations, 2 with a weight."

Each Location the batch is at has its remaining weight there, a bar, and one line, such as "4180 g entered 25 Nov by Sam. Since: 38 Shots used 760 g, 500 g moved to Riverside." Where a move brought the batch to a Location that had no weight for it, the line starts with what arrived, such as "500 g arrived from Downtown 26 Nov." The line's count of Shots opens those Shots in the Shots list.

The bar shows what is left of the coffee that stayed at that Location: its remaining weight, against that weight and what the Shots pulled there since have used. Coffee moved on to another Location, or received from one, is not drawn as used, so the bar is full until a Shot is pulled and empty at 0 g. A Location that does not track the batch has no bar.

The Bean Batches list shows the same weights, a line for each Location under **At**, and the total under **Remaining in all**.

- Each Location has its own weight. A Shot lowers only the weight at the Location it was pulled at. A sample kept at the lab and the bucket at a cafe are one batch with two weights.
- Every Shot pulled with the batch at that Location after the weight was entered, Unfinished Shots and Trial Shots too, counts, since the coffee was ground. A Shot from before the entry is already in the weight you entered. A Shot counts its measured dose where it recorded one, otherwise the dose its Workflow was set to, otherwise nothing.
- The weight follows the Shots. A Shot that arrives late from a tablet that was offline still counts, and correcting a Shot's dose corrects the weight.
- Entering a weight starts that Location's count again: Shots and moves from before it no longer bear on the number. A move of part of a batch does not, so a Shot pulled before the move that arrives after it still counts where it was pulled.
- A batch with no weight entered at a Location reads **Not tracked** there, never zero, and Shots take nothing from it. It can be tracked at one Location and not at another. To stop tracking, press **Enter weight**, leave the field empty and save.
- Shots never take a weight below zero, and reaching zero that way finishes nothing: the batch stays where it is, as the weight entered was too low. Entering a weight of zero yourself says the bucket is empty, so it finishes the batch there, as **Finish here** does. The dialog says so before you save, its button reads **Finish here**, and it asks nothing more. The batch's history keeps it as a finish made by a weight of zero.
- Each tablet at a Location shows the batch's remaining weight there, kept up to date after each Shot and each move; a tablet that was offline catches up when it reconnects.
- A barista enters the day's weight at the Machine, on a tablet running the [Streamline Cafe Skin](https://github.com/loganfuller/streamline-cafe-js): in the Skin's bean and batch dialog, highlight the batch, press **Enter weight**, type what the scale reads and save. It is a weight entered at that Machine's Location, as one entered here is, and starts the count there again. The batch's history names the Machine it was entered on and no account, as a tablet has no sign-in. It arrives with the tablet's next report, every 30 seconds unless its plugin's poll interval was changed, or once the tablet reconnects where the server could not be reached.
- Where the weight entered in the Skin leaves nothing, its button reads "Finish batch" and the batch is finished at that Location, as **Finish here** does.
- A remaining weight changed on a tablet any other way, as in DYE2, is entered for that tablet's Location too, and one set to zero finishes the batch there.
- Coffee used outside a Shot is not counted, such as purging a grinder or a bag sold over the counter. Weigh the bucket and enter the weight to correct for it.

Staff enter a weight and finish a batch only at the Locations they work at. They move a batch when the Location it leaves or the one it joins is one they work at, so whoever sends a bucket or receives it can record the move. A scan, a weight entered or a batch finished on a tablet needs no account: each bears only on that tablet's own Location.

### See what a batch is for, and what a Location has

A batch's page has a **Recipes** card: every Recipe that names the batch, at any Location, with its Location, its Recipe Slot or "No slot", and its dose and yield. Open it before a move or a finish to see what that affects.

- A Recipe at a Location the batch was finished at, or moved away from entirely, is still listed, marked **Batch finished there** beside the Location's name, until someone picks another batch for it.
- A Recipe that names another batch of the same Bean is not listed, and neither is an Archived Recipe.

A Location's page has a **Bean Batches** card: the batches there now, the latest roasted first. A batch finished there, moved away entirely or Archived is not in it. Each row gives:

- the Bean over the day it was roasted, such as "Roasted 17 Nov", and the Batch Code;
- the remaining weight there, with the weight its count starts from, such as "of 4180 g entered 25 Nov", or **Not tracked** where no weight was entered there, never zero;
- the coffee used and the number of Shots pulled there since the count started, or "No Shots since", and what was moved in and away since;
- the Recipes at that Location that name the batch, each with its Recipe Slot;
- **Enter weight**, for Admins and for Staff who work at that Location.

Everything in a row is that Location's own. A batch at two Locations is in each one's card, with that Location's weight, Shots and moves. A row links to the batch, to each of its Recipes there, and to the batch's Shots at that Location in the Shots list.

Nothing is changed from a batch's **Recipes** card. A Location's card changes only a batch's weight there, with **Enter weight**, which is on a row that reads **Not tracked** too, as that is how tracking starts there. A weight of zero finishes the batch at that Location, and its row leaves the card. Move a batch, or finish it without weighing it, from the batch's **Where it is** card. Viewers read both cards, and Staff read every Location's.

## Choose what a Location offers

A Location offers the Bean Batches that are there and the Profiles, Baskets and Tools it shows. A Bean has no setting of its own: it is offered wherever one of its batches is, so finishing a Bean's last batch at a Location stops offering the Bean there. A Bean with no batch at a Location yet is also offered there where a tablet created it, linked a Bean of its own to it, or restored it, until a batch is added.

### Show or hide a Profile, Basket or Tool

1. Open the Profile, Basket or Tool from its tab of the **Library**.
2. In its **Locations** card, turn the **Shown** switch on or off in the Location's row.

Showing it writes it to that Location's tablets. Hiding a Profile hides it on them without deleting it, a Basket is archived in DYE2's list, and a Tool is removed from it. None of this changes another Location. The Machines at a Location use the Baskets and Tools it shows.

A Profile reaches only Machines that can run it: showing one at a Location with a Machine whose model cannot run it is refused, naming the Machine.

A Grinder is not shown or hidden. It belongs to one Location, chosen when it is created, and is written to that Location's tablets only.

## Enter and edit coffee and equipment

Anything a barista enters on a tablet joins the Library by itself. You can enter and edit the same things here.

### Add a Bean and its first batch

1. Open **Library**, then **Beans**, and press **New Bean**.
2. Fill in its **Roaster** and **Name**, and what else you know about it, then press **Create**.
3. On the Bean's page press **New Batch**, in the heading of its **Batches** card. Give its **Roast date**, its **Batch Code** if you number your roasts, the **Location** it is at, and its **Roasted weight (g)**. Press **Create**.

The batch, with its Bean, is written to the tablets at that Location. Given a roasted weight, its remaining weight there starts at that weight; type another under **Remaining weight (g)** if some of it is already gone. A batch is created at one Location, so its weight is counted once, and reaches another by [a move](#move-a-bean-batch-to-another-location-or-finish-it-there).

### See a Bean's batches

A Bean's page leads with its **Batches** card, its roasts with the latest first. Beside the card's title is how many of them are at a Location and what those Locations hold in all, such as "4 at a Location, 17090 g in all", or "none with a weight" where no Location tracks one.

| Column | What it gives |
|---|---|
| **Roasted** | The day the batch was roasted, which opens the batch. An Archived batch reads **Archived** beside it. |
| **Batch Code** | The batch's Batch Code, or a dash. |
| **At** | Each Location the batch is at, with its remaining weight there, or "At no Location". |
| **Remaining in all** | The sum over the Locations that track it. |

### How a batch is named

Wherever the management interface mentions a batch, it names it by its Bean and its Batch Code, such as House Espresso `LR-0411`.

- A batch with no Batch Code is named by its roast date instead, such as "House Espresso, roasted 10 Nov".
- A roast date is written as a day, with its year when that is not this year. It is the day entered, in whatever time zone the page is read.
- A name is a link to the batch's page. In a dialog or a list of choices, which can't link, it is written out in full, such as "House Espresso LR-0411, roasted 17 Nov".
- An assistant, and a message from the server, name it the same way, with its roast date written as 2026-11-17: "House Espresso LR-0411, roasted 2026-11-17".

### Give a batch its Batch Code

A **Batch Code** is your own identifier for a roast, as on its label, such as `PR-0470`. It is free text of up to 100 characters, in any format, and a batch need not have one.

- Give it in the **New Batch** dialog, or open the batch, press **Edit** on its **Roast** card, and fill in or change **Batch Code**. Emptying it clears it. Each change is in the batch's history.
- One code means one batch. A code that another batch holds is refused, with a link to that batch, whatever its case and whatever spaces surround it: `pr-0470` can't be entered beside `PR-0470`.
- An Archived batch's code is free to give to another batch. Restoring the Archived batch is then refused until one of the two has another code.
- On the **Bean Batches** tab the **Batch Code** column shows each batch's code, and the search beside **New Batch** finds a batch by its code, its Bean or its roaster.
- The code is written to the tablets that hold the batch, where a Skin can show it. It is not set on a tablet: one changed there is put back.

### Add a Grinder, Basket or Tool

- **Grinder**: on the **Grinders** tab press **New Grinder**, choose its **Location** and give its **Model**. Its **Setting type** is **Numeric** for a numbered dial or **Preset values** for named positions. It may also hold the range of its setting and RPM and a default for each, which a Machine takes as a barista picks the Grinder on it.
- **Basket**: on the **Baskets** tab press the button for a new Basket, choose a Location under **Shown at** and give its **Name**. One Basket stands for every copy of that model, so its name is used once in the Library: show it at the other Locations that have one.
- **Tool**: on the **Tools** tab press the button for a new Tool, and go on in the same way. A Tool is anything else a Recipe or Shot can name, such as a tamper or a puck screen, with attributes you name yourself.

### Edit an item

Open the item and press **Edit** on the card that holds what you want to change: **Coffee** on a Bean's page, **Roast** on a Bean Batch's, **Grinder**, **Basket** or **Tool** on theirs. Press **Save**. The change is written to every tablet that holds the item.

A Profile's **Title, author and notes** card is edited the same way. Changing its steps is different, and is covered in [Profiles and Drafts](../profiles-and-drafts/).

## Recipes and Recipe Slots

A Recipe is a named, saved dial-in for a coffee: its Bean Batch, Profile, dose, yield, Grinder and grind setting, Basket and Tools, and any steam, hot water and flush settings it holds. It belongs to one Location, where any Machine can load it.

Each Location has nine Recipe Slots, 1 to 9, shared by every Machine there. On a tablet:

- Streamline's DYE strip has a button for each of slots 1 to 5.
- A Recipe in slots 6 to 9 is a button on a Skin that shows nine, and DYE2 lists it.
- A Recipe in no slot is kept at its Location and found on each tablet there in DYE2's Select Recipe page.

A Recipe is in at most one slot. Taking a Recipe in slots 1 to 5 off any Machine's strip takes it out of its slot for the whole Location.

<Still name="location" alt="Downtown's page: its Overview block, with a status card for each of its two Machines, and its Recipes card, with four Recipes in Recipe Slots 1 to 4, each with its Bean Batch, Profile, dose and yield, Grinder setting and Reference Shot" />

A Location's Recipes are on its own page, in the **Recipes** card, and every Location's are together on the Library's **Recipes** tab. Each row names the Recipe's Bean Batch by its Bean and Batch Code, over the day it was roasted, and links to the batch. **Batch finished here** beside the batch marks one that is no longer at the Location, and **Archived** beside a Recipe's name an Archived Recipe.

### Create a Recipe

1. Open **Library**, then **Recipes**, and press **New Recipe**. Choose its **Location**.
2. Give it a **Name**, and choose what it holds: its **Bean Batch**, **Profile**, **Dose (g)** and **Yield (g)**, **Grinder** and **Grind setting**, **Basket** and **Tools**.
3. Under **Recipe Slot**, choose **The next free slot**, **A slot of my choosing**, or **No slot: kept here only**.
4. Press **Create**.

**Bean Batch** is a field you type into. Type part of the Bean's name, its roaster or its Batch Code, and the list under it shows the batches that match:

- **At** the Location, the latest roasted first, each with its roast day and what is left of it there, such as "3240 g here", or "Not tracked".
- **Elsewhere: saving adds it at** the Location, each with the Locations it is at and what they hold of it, such as "6000 g there".
- **No batch**.

Press a batch to choose it, or move through the list with the arrow keys and press Enter. A batch picked from elsewhere is added at the Location with no weight. To bring its weight, move it on the batch's page ([Move a Bean Batch to another Location, or finish it there](#move-a-bean-batch-to-another-location-or-finish-it-there)).

The Profile and Basket lists offer what the Location has first, and choosing a Profile, Basket or Tool not shown there shows it there. The form says what saving adds or shows under **Saving also** before you save.

A Recipe created on a tablet, in DYE2, joins the Library the same way.

### Edit a Recipe

Open the Recipe and press **Edit**. Each field you change is written to every tablet at its Location, and reaches every Machine that has the Recipe loaded ([How a dial-in change travels](#how-a-dial-in-change-travels)).

<Still name="recipe-details" alt="A Recipe's Recipe card: its Bean Batch, Profile, dose, yield, Grinder, grind setting and Basket, with a button to show the fields not set" />

The Recipe's page lists what it holds in its **Recipe** card, with a button under them to show the fields not set.

### Change a Recipe's Bean Batch

Picking the next roast is the commonest edit of a Recipe, so its page leads with its batch. Under the Recipe's name, the **Bean Batch** strip gives the batch, linked, with its Batch Code, its roast day and how long ago that was, and its roaster. Beside it, **Remaining at** the Location is what is left of the batch there, or "Not tracked" where no weight is entered.

1. Press **Change batch**.
2. Type part of the Bean's name, its roaster or its Batch Code, and choose the batch from the list, as in the Recipe form.
3. Press **Save**. **Cancel** changes nothing.

Saving changes the Recipe's batch and nothing else. As any edit, it reaches every tablet at the Location and every Machine that has the Recipe loaded, and the Recipe's **History** keeps it.

When the batch is finished at the Recipe's Location, or moved away from it, the Recipe reads **Batch finished here** and the strip says so, with the day. Under **Pick the next roast**, it lists the same Bean's other batches at the Location, the latest roasted first, each with what is left of it there:

- **Use this batch** changes the Recipe's batch at once, with nothing to confirm.
- **Another batch…** opens the same dialog as **Change batch**, for a batch of another Bean or from another Location.

Admins, and Staff who work at the Recipe's Location, see these buttons. Everyone else sees the strip without them.

### Move a Recipe between slots

1. Open the Recipe and press **Move to a slot**, or **Put in a slot** for one in none.
2. Choose **The next free slot** or **A slot of my choosing**, then press **Move**.

If the slot you choose holds another Recipe, the two swap, or the other is taken out of its slot and kept at the Location. The dialog says which before you move it. The Recipe keeps its history.

To empty its slot, open **More** and choose **Take out of its slot**.

### Copy a Recipe to another Location

Open **More** and choose **Copy to a Location**. Choose where under **Copy to**, and its slot, Grinder and Bean Batch there, then press **Copy**. **Bean Batch** is the same field you type into: its first entry keeps the Recipe's own batch, and under **None** are the batches at the Location you copy to.

The copy is a new Recipe at that Location, a starting point for dialling in there. It keeps the dose, yield, grind setting and the steam, hot water and flush settings, and starts with the same Reference Shot. Its history starts with the copy, and the Recipe you copied is not changed.

## How a dial-in change travels

The Recipe a barista last applied on a Machine is that Machine's Loaded Recipe. It stays loaded while they dial it in, until they apply another Recipe or one of Streamline's favourites, or change the Profile.

- From a Machine to the Recipe: a change made on a Machine is saved into its Loaded Recipe: a new grind setting, dose or yield, or another Bean Batch picked there.
- From the Recipe to every Machine: an edit to a Recipe, made on a tablet or here, reaches every Machine that has it loaded, at once.

So when the barista on one Machine goes a step finer for the afternoon, the other Machines with that Recipe loaded follow, and the Recipe's page shows the new setting.

Dialling values in by hand without applying a Recipe loads nothing, and nothing is saved into a Recipe.

## What loading a Recipe sets

A Recipe is the whole Shot. A Machine that loads one takes everything it names, and is left with none of what it names none of: a Recipe with no Grinder or Basket leaves the Machine with no Grinder or Basket, and the same goes for its Bean Batch, grind setting, dose and yield.

Steam, hot water and flush settings are the exception. A Machine takes each one the Recipe holds and keeps its own for each one the Recipe leaves empty. In the Recipe's form these are under **Steam**, **Hot water** and **Flush**.

The Profile is the other exception: a Recipe that names none leaves the Machine with the Profile it has.

Steam is turned on or off as the Recipe loads:

- A Recipe that holds a **Steam temperature** turns the Machine's steam on at that temperature.
- A steam temperature of 0 is **Off**, which turns steam off on every Machine that has the Recipe loaded.

Machines at one Location with different Recipes loaded can therefore steam differently. Turning steam off at the Machine never changes the Recipe.

## A Recipe's Reference Shot

A Reference Shot is the one Shot chosen to show how a Recipe's Shots should go. It can be any Shot: the one you just dialled in, or one from another Machine or Location, such as the lab's, on the Recipe's Profile or another.

<Still name="recipe" alt="House Espresso's page: its Reference Shot card, with small pressure, flow and weight curves, its duration, yield against target, rating and grind setting, and buttons to open it, choose another or clear it; beside it, today's Shots by Machine, each against the Reference Shot" />

To choose one, open the Shot and press **Make it a Reference Shot**, choose the **Recipe**, and press **Make it the Reference Shot**. In the Recipes lists, a Recipe without one says **No reference**, which opens its rated Shots to choose from.

The Recipe's **Reference Shot** card shows the Shot's pressure, flow and weight curves in small, with its duration, its yield against its target, its rating and its grind setting. It says where the Shot's Profile is another than the Recipe's. From the card you can **Open** the Shot, **Choose another**, or **Clear** it.

- It stays until someone chooses another or clears it, so editing the Recipe's grind, yield, Bean Batch or Profile leaves it in place.
- The Recipe's **History** keeps who chose it. A Skin on a tablet at the Recipe's Location may choose it too, and the history then names the Machine it was chosen on.
- Each tablet at the Recipe's Location keeps a copy for a Skin to draw, while the Recipe is in a Recipe Slot.
- The Shots list marks the Shot **Reference**.

The Recipe's other Shots are read against it in these places:

- A Shot's page draws the Reference Shot's curves behind the Shot's own.
- The Overview says how far each Machine's median duration is from the Reference Shot's.
- The Recipe's page does the same beside each Machine's numbers, in **By Machine**, and draws the Reference Shot's duration across the **Trend**.
- A Machine's page does the same for each Recipe it pulled, in its **Shots** card.
- Each row of the Shots list gives the Shot's duration against the Reference Shot's.
- A Shot Summary grouped by Recipe, through the REST API or an assistant, gives the Reference Shot's numbers beside the Recipe's.

A Shot's duration, or a group's median duration, more than 10% from the Reference Shot's is out of spec. [Shots and out of spec](../shots/) covers what that means.

## History and Lost Edits

Two people can edit the same item at once, on two tablets or on a tablet and here. Edits merge field by field: a change to a Recipe's grind setting and a change to its yield both apply. Where two edits change the same field, the one made most recently wins.

Every item keeps its history. Open the item and find its **History** card, which lists each change that was kept, the latest first, with when it was made and the Machine or account it came from. On a Recipe's page the card is closed until you press **Show**.

An edit that lost to another made without seeing it is not thrown away. It is kept in the history as a Lost Edit, marked **Lost Edit**, with the value it lost to and who made that change. A Lost Edit is also kept when a barista enters on a tablet something the Library already has, such as a Bean of the same roaster and name, with values that differ. A tablet joining a Location keeps none: the Location's values are written over its own.

A Lost Edit is for you to read: nothing needs doing unless the value that lost was the right one. To bring it back, edit the item and enter that value again.

## Archive or delete

Archive is the ordinary way to retire something. Delete is for a mistake.

| | Archive | Delete |
|---|---|---|
| Who | An Admin, or Staff: a Recipe only at a Location they work at | An Admin |
| What happens | The item is offered nowhere, and kept, so past Shots still name it. | The item is gone from the Library, with its history, and from every tablet that holds it, now or once it connects. |
| Undone by | **Restore** | Nothing |
| Allowed for | Any item | Only an item no Shot names |

On a tablet, an Archived Bean, Bean Batch, Grinder or Profile is archived or hidden, and an Archived Recipe, Basket or Tool is removed from DYE2's lists until it is restored.

To archive an item, open it and press **Archive**. For a Recipe or a Bean Batch, open **More** beside its name and choose **Archive**. The Recipes tab hides archived Recipes until you tick **Show archived**.

To delete an item, open it and press **Delete**, or choose **Delete** under **More** on a Recipe or a Bean Batch. The rules that keep a delete safe:

- An item a Shot names cannot be deleted. Archive it instead.
- A Basket or Tool cannot be deleted while a Recipe names it either.
- A Profile, Grinder or Bean Batch that a Recipe names can be deleted while no Shot names it. The Recipe is left naming something the Library no longer has, so check its Recipes first.
- Deleting a Bean deletes its batches with it.
- A Profile bundled with Decaid is never deleted, as every tablet has it. Hide it at a Location or archive it.

Deleting on a tablet is different, and safe: it only takes the item away at that tablet's Location. The Library keeps it, and so do the other Locations.
