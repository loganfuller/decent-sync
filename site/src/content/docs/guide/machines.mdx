Each Machine has a page of its own, in five tabs. This page covers the Machines list and what each tab is for: watching a Machine, seeing how it was used, changing its settings, managing what its Tablet runs, and its setup.

Beside its Shots and Steam Records, a Machine's Tablet reports its Workflow changes, its Machine States, its settings and its paired devices, and Decent Sync keeps each. The words in capitals, such as Machine State, Settings Edit and Tablet Software, are defined in the [Glossary](../../reference/glossary/).

The management interface fits a phone's screen, in light or dark, so all of this works away from a desk.

## See all the Machines

Open **Machines**. The list gives, for each Machine:

- its Location;
- its Status, which is whether it is online;
- its Machine State, which is what it is doing;
- its Last Shot, marked where it was [out of spec](../../guide/shots/#what-out-of-spec-means);
- its Loaded Recipe, with its Recipe Slot;
- under Attention, anything that needs it, such as a refused connection.

<Still name="machines" alt="The Machines list: four Machines, each with its Location, its Status, its Machine State, its Last Shot with its Recipe and duration, and its Loaded Recipe with its Recipe Slot" />

Choose a Machine to open its page. Adding a Machine, and what its Token is for, are in [Locations and Machines](../../get-started/locations-and-machines/).

## Find your way around a Machine's page

A Machine's page opens on **Now**, with **Activity**, **Settings**, **Software** and **Setup** beside it. The tab is part of the page's address, so a link opens on the tab it names.

Above the tabs, whichever is open:

- the Machine's name, and whether it is online;
- what it is doing;
- its last Shot, marked where it was out of spec;
- its Location;
- links to its **Shots** and its **Steam Records**;
- anything that needs attention, such as a refused connection.

| To | Open |
|---|---|
| Watch the Machine, or see how its Shots are landing | [Now](#watch-a-machine-now) |
| See how much it was used, or what was changed on it | [Activity](#see-how-a-machine-was-used) |
| Change its settings, or see what changed them | [Settings](#change-a-machines-settings) |
| Install, update or remove a plugin or a Skin, or update Decaid | [Software](#manage-what-its-tablet-runs) |
| Check its hardware, move it, or issue a new Token | [Setup](#check-a-machines-setup) |

## Watch a Machine now

The **Now** tab has three cards.

<Still name="live-view" alt="A Machine's Live View during a shot: its Machine State, temperatures, pressure, flow and scale, and the shot's curves drawn as it runs" />

- **Live View** shows what the Machine is doing and measuring, and draws the curves of a shot or of steaming as it runs. It is live only while the page is open, and nothing in it is stored.
- **Workflow** shows what the Machine is set up to pull next, with the Loaded Recipe it was loaded from. Its **Bean Batch** row names the batch by its Bean and Batch Code, with the day it was roasted and its remaining weight at the Machine's Location, or **Batch finished here** where the batch is no longer there.
- **Shots** shows how its Shots are landing, Recipe by Recipe. Choose Today, Yesterday or 7 days. Each Recipe's row gives how many Shots there were, their median duration against the Recipe's Reference Shot's, their yield against target and the share stopped at target, marked where out of spec. A Recipe's count opens those Shots in the Shots list.

## See how a Machine was used

The **Activity** tab is the Machine's Machine Activity: how much it was used, then what happened on it over one day.

<Still name="machine-activity" alt="A Machine's Activity tab: the Use card, charting its Shots by day over 14 days with the time in each Machine State under it, and the On one day card, open on the Dial-in trail" />

### Use, by day and by hour

The **Use** card charts how many Shots, Steam Records, flushes and hot water pours the Machine made, with how long it spent in each Machine State: idle, asleep, needing water and the rest.

1. Choose **By day** to chart each day. It opens on the last 14 days. Set **From** and **To** for any range of up to 31 days.
2. Choose **By hour** to chart each hour of one day, and set the **Day**.

- A day is the Machine's Location's own.
- For now, a Machine whose Tablet was off counts as in its last Machine State until the Tablet is back.

### What happened on one day

Under Use, the **On one day** card lists one day's activity, latest first. It shows today unless you choose another day: set its **Day**, or choose that day's bar in the Use chart.

| Choose | To see |
|---|---|
| Dial-in trail | Each Recipe loaded or unloaded, and what was changed between them, from what to what and by whom. The Recipe, Profile, Bean Batch, Grinder and Basket it names are links. A change to the next roast of the same Bean names both batches by their Batch Codes, and a change of Bean names each batch by its Bean and Batch Code. |
| Loaded Recipes | The Recipes the Machine had loaded that day, with when each was loaded and unloaded. |
| Machine States | Each Machine State it went through. |

## Change a Machine's settings

The **Settings** tab holds the Machine Settings, which are its firmware's, and the Tablet Settings, which are Decaid's own on its Tablet, wake schedules included.

<Still name="machine-settings" alt="A Machine's Settings tab: its Machine Settings in the groups General, Advanced, Flow calibration and Sensor calibration, each with Edit" />

A change made here is a Settings Edit. It can be made only while the Machine is online and its Tablet connected.

1. Open the Machine's **Settings** tab.
2. Choose **Edit** beside the group to change, such as General or Tablet Settings. A sensor calibration is edited one value at a time. Under Wake schedules, choose **Add** for a new one.
3. Change the values, then choose **Save**.

The tab then says what came of the edit: Pending while it waits, then Applied, Stored otherwise where the Machine holds another value than was asked for, Not read back where Decaid took the change but its values could not be read back, or why it was refused. Not confirmed means the Machine didn't answer within a minute, or its connection closed first: a write already under way may still finish, and an answer that arrives later is shown. A few settings only an Admin can change.

The tab also lists the Machine's **Paired devices**, and under **Steam, hot water and rinse** the steam, hot water and flush settings its Workflow holds. Decaid calls the flush settings rinse.

### See what changed, and who changed it

The **Settings History** card, on the same tab, lists every change of the Machine's settings, plugins and their settings, Skins, paired devices and sensors, whatever made it.

- A change made by a Settings Edit or a Software Action names who made it and what came of it.
- A change made on the Tablet, or by anything else outside Decent Sync, is listed too, tied to no person.
- A Settings Edit that was refused or not confirmed is listed too.
- Choose **Settings Edits** in place of **All changes** to narrow the list to Settings Edits alone.

## Manage what its Tablet runs

The **Software** tab shows the Tablet Software: Decaid, and every plugin and Skin on the Tablet.

<Still name="machine-software" alt="A Machine's Software tab: the Decaid card with its version, its update and its automatic update checks, the Plugins card with each plugin's version, how it updates and its state, and the Skins card with what the Tablet shows and each Skin's version and how it updates" />

- **Decaid** shows its version, the update it offers, and whether its automatic update checks are on.
- **Plugins** and **Skins** list each one with its version and how it updates: Pinned, following releases or a branch, or not updated.
- The Skins card says which Skin build the Tablet is showing and since when, for a Skin that reports its page, and says when a newer one is installed.

Each thing asked of the Tablet from this tab, but for turning its automatic update checks on or off, is a Software Action.

- It is made only while the Tablet is connected and, for all but the check for Decaid's update, the Machine idle.
- It is made once, never kept for later.
- It is logged in the **Software Actions** card with who asked and what came of it.

An Admin can make every Software Action. Staff and Viewers read the tab.

### Plugins

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Install a plugin | Choose **Install plugin**, give its GitHub repository, and choose its latest release, or a branch or a commit. A commit stays Pinned. A plugin newly installed is not enabled. |
| Enable or disable one | Open its row's menu and choose **Enable** or **Disable**. |
| Configure one | Choose **Settings** on its row. The fields are the ones the plugin declares. |
| Change how one updates | Open its row's menu and choose **Change how it updates…**, then follow releases or a branch, or pin it where it is now or to a commit. |
| Check the plugins for updates | Choose **Check for updates** on the Plugins card. |
| Approve an update that waits on new permissions | Choose **Approve** on its row. |
| Remove one | Open its row's menu and choose **Remove**. |

### Skins

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Install a Skin | Choose **Install Skin**, and install it from a repository's latest release, a branch or a URL. |
| Choose the default Skin | Choose **Make default** on its row. |
| Change how one updates | Open its row's menu and choose **Change how it updates…**. |
| Check the Skins for updates | Choose **Check for updates** on the Skins card. |
| Remove one | Open its row's menu and choose **Remove**. |
| Have the Skin on screen load its page again | Choose **Reload**. It is offered for a Skin that reports its page. |
| Rebuild the view on the default Skin | Choose **Restart the Skin view**. It works for any Skin. |

Reload and Restart the Skin view are the two Software Actions that are not an Admin's alone: Admins, Staff at the Machine's Location, and an assistant acting for either may ask for them.

### Decaid

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Ask Decaid to check for its own update | Choose **Check now**. |
| Start Decaid's update | Choose **Start the update on the Tablet**. Someone at the Tablet must then confirm it in Android's installer and tap **Open**: Decaid does not reopen by itself, and the Machine is offline until it does. |
| Turn the automatic update checks on or off | Choose **Edit** beside Automatic update checks. |

Turning the automatic update checks on or off is a Settings Edit, not a Software Action: it is listed with the Machine's Settings Edits and in its Settings History, on the **Settings** tab. Decaid follows it when it next starts.

On a phone the plugins, the Skins and the log are lists, each row's actions are in one menu, and nothing scrolls sideways.

## Check a Machine's setup

The **Setup** tab holds what is looked at seldom.

- **Hardware and software**: the Machine's hardware, its firmware and its Tablets, with a link to the Software tab for what the Tablet runs.
- **Location**: where the Machine is, and its Location History, which is where it has been.
- **Sharing**: whether the Machine shares the Library. An Admin turns it off or on with **Share the Library**. A Machine that does not share is a Capture-only Machine: its Shots and Steam Records are still recorded.
- **Token**: the Machine's Token, for an Admin, who can issue a new one there.
